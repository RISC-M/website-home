We wouldn't be here without your support.

Sponsor organizations play an essential role in our organizational development. Through design reviews, tool support, and material support, sponsors help us achieve our goals and recreate an industry-adjacent environment with the resources we have at hand.

We Create Great Engineers.

We provide a unique opportunity for students to gain specialized, industry-relevant skills before they enter the full-time workforce. RISC-M members will have completed demonstrable and verifiable projects that will ensure they can hit the ground running in professional roles.
