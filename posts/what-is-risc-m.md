## What is RISC-M?

RISC-M is a computer architecture club at the University of Michigan. We bring
students together to work on hardware projects, share what they are learning,
and explore computer architecture outside the classroom.

## Why we started

After taking EECS 470 and building a foundation in computer architecture, we
wanted a place to keep going. We saw an opportunity for a student community
where people could collaborate on projects, get feedback, and learn from one
another.

RISC-M grew out of that idea: a place to turn an interest in computer hardware
into something you can design, build, and explain.

## What this showcase is for

This page is where members can share what they are working on. Each project
gets space for the problem, the design, and the decisions behind it, along with
links to the people and code involved.

Projects do not have to be finished to belong here. Sharing work in progress
lets other members follow along, ask questions, and offer feedback while there
is still room to try something different.
