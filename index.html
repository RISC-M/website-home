RISC-M

The U-M
Computer Architecture
Club

A student-led community dedicated to computer hardware & silicon design.

As Michigan’s first student organization for computer architecture, we provide a collaborative space to explore hardware beyond the classroom. We work on exciting projects, invite industry guest speakers, and host events. Beyond our own projects, we are dedicated to helping our fellow students succeed by sharing knowledge and resources, assisting with debugging, and providing guidance for students interested in the hardware industry.

Membership

Who Should Join?

We welcome anyone passionate about computer architecture who has completed or is currently enrolled in EECS 470 or 472+. Whether you want to gain practical experience building projects in Verilog, need some help debugging a tough lab, want to hear from professionals, or are looking for recruiting advice, you will find a supportive community here.
