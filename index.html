Michigan’s first dedicated organization for hardware systems. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and industrial application, fostering a community focused on advanced processor design and hardware-software co-design.

Explore the internals of EDA tools, the complexities of modern microarchitecture, and collaborate on ambitious systems projects.

Membership Requirements

Who Should Join?

We welcome anyone passionate about computer architecture who has completed or is currently enrolled in EECS 470 or 472+. Whether you want to gain practical experience with Verilog, engage in research projects, or assist in debugging architecture implementations, you will find a community here.

Our Initiatives

Our immediate roadmap for the club includes:

  • Architecture Projects & Research Papers
  • Debug Nights (Mentorship for 470/472)
  • Hardware Competitions
  • Potential Industry Pipeline (Speakers & Alumni Networking)
