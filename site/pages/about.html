Our Vision

As sophomores, we noticed the lack of a dedicated computer architecture club on campus, and we realized how much students needed a community like this during their time at Michigan.

After completing EECS 470, the computer architecture course and building our foundational knowledge, our vision became clear: to build a space where students interested in computer architecture can come together, collaborate, receive feedback, and work on real, interesting projects outside of coursework.
