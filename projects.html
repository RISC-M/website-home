UMARV Collaboration

Perception Preprocessor

Active
May 2026 — Present

Accelerating UMARV LiDAR perception on an FPGA fabric to minimize latency for autonomous navigation.

This dedicated signal processor implements coordinate transformations, ground plane filtering, and occupancy grid mapping in Verilog to bridge the gap between raw sensor outputs and the embedded software stack.
