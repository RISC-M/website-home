// 01 ABOUT THE CLUB

About Us.

As Michigan’s first student organization for computer architecture, we provide a collaborative space to explore computer hardware and silicon design beyond the classroom. We build ambitious processor architectures, invite industry guest speakers, and host technical events. Beyond our own projects, we are dedicated to helping our fellow students succeed by sharing knowledge and resources, assisting with lab debugging, and providing guidance for students interested in the hardware and semiconductor industry.

MEMBERSHIP

Who Should Join?

We welcome anyone passionate about computer architecture who has completed or is currently enrolled in EECS 470 or 472+.

Whether you want to gain practical experience building projects in SystemVerilog, need help debugging a tough pipeline lab, want to hear from industry professionals, or are looking for recruiting advice, you will find a supportive community here.

Prerequisites & Background
EECS 470 EECS 472+ SystemVerilog Hardware Labs
INITIATIVES

Our Core Initiatives

  • •
    Debug Nights & Course Help: Peer mentorship and collaborative debugging for EECS 470/472 labs and homework.
  • •
    Hands-On Hardware Projects: Custom processor architectures, cycle-accurate simulators, and ASIC synthesis.
  • •
    Industry Guest Speakers: Technical talks and Q&As with leading architects from top semiconductor companies.
  • •
    Recruiting & Career Support: Technical interview prep, resume reviews, and semiconductor alumni networking.
  • •
    Resource & Knowledge Sharing: Architecture papers, tool guides, FPGA tutorials, and design resources.

// 02 JOIN THE TEAM

Application Process.

Joining RISC-M is straightforward. We review applications on a rolling basis and look for passionate builders excited about computer architecture and silicon design.

01 / APPLICATION

Apply Online

Submit our short application form and answer a few simple questions regarding your background, coursework, and hardware interests.

02 / INTERVIEW

10–15 Min Chat

If you meet our qualifications, you’ll have a quick, informal 10–15 minute conversation with an executive board member to align on goals.

03 / ONBOARDING

Team Placement

Get placed onto an active silicon project, research stream, or architecture cohort and start building hardware with the team.
