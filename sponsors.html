Partner With Us

As Michigan's first student organization dedicated to computer architecture, we are building a collaborative community that explores hardware design beyond the classroom.

By partnering with RISC-M, your company gains direct access to a dedicated group of students who are actively building hands-on projects, sharing engineering knowledge, and preparing for careers in the silicon industry. Your sponsorship directly supports our mission to help our fellow students succeed through project funding and industry networking.

Why Sponsor?

Community Connections

Host exclusive tech talks, debugging workshops, and recruiting events with students deeply passionate about hardware and silicon design.

Project Funding

Support hands-on learning by funding FPGA acquisitions, student-led designs, and our overarching mission to provide practical engineering experience.

Brand Visibility

Feature your company logo prominently on our website, project repositories, and our community apparel.
